How the AI works

An AI that lives in my house.

No cloud, no account. It runs on a small Raspberry Pi at home and learns only from me.

I listen

Every song I like, finish or skip tells it a little more about my taste.

It learns

It scores songs, artists and styles, and what I listened to lately counts most, so it changes when my taste changes.

It plays

When the playlist ends, it picks the next songs and builds my mood playlists by itself.
