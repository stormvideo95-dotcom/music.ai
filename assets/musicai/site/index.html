Music AI Made by Storm Gees

I don't pay for Spotify. I built my own.

Music AI is my own music app for my phone and my Mac. It plays any song without ads, and a small computer at home learns what I like, so the music keeps going when my playlist ends.

Everything I wanted from a music app.

It keeps playing

When my playlist ends, it carries on with new songs in the same style.

Playlists for my mood

My liked songs sort themselves into Phonk and Everything else.

Phone, Mac and TV

I can move a song from my phone to my Mac at the same second, or cast it to the TV.

Same likes everywhere

A song I like on my phone is liked on my Mac too.

Lyrics that follow

The line being sung lights up and scrolls along with the song.

In the car too

It works with Android Auto, so the same music plays on the way.

The brain is a Raspberry Pi in my house.

Nothing goes to a cloud. It only learns from me.

  1. I listen

    Every song I like, finish or skip is sent home.

  3. The Pi learns

    It scores songs, artists and styles. What I played lately counts most.

  5. The next song plays

    It picks songs I'll like and keeps my mood playlists up to date.
